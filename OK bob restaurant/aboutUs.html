About Us

OK Bob is a restaurant created by
Chefs Decker Gokioco and Jorge
Mendez, together with their team, the
Tasteless Food Group. Their goal is to
bring flavorful and satisfying Middle
Eastern-inspired dishes to customers in
the Philippines.

Our Food

From delicious shawarma and kebabs
to flavorful biryani, curries, and other
specialties, OK Bob offers a variety of
dishes made for different tastes and
occasions.
